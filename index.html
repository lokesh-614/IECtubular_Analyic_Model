Mathematical Formulation & Numerical Methodology

Cross-Flow Indirect Evaporative Cooler (IEC) Governing Equations

1 Cross-Flow Indirect Evaporative Cooling Mechanism

In a tubular cross-flow Indirect Evaporative Cooler (IEC), heat and mass transfer occur across two unmixed air streams separated by the tube wall:

  • Primary Air (Product Stream, $x$-direction): Flows inside the tubes of inner diameter $D_i$ and length $L$. It undergoes purely sensible cooling at constant humidity ratio ($\omega_{p,\text{out}} = \omega_{p,\text{in}}$).
  • Secondary Air (Working Stream, $y$-direction): Flows perpendicular across the exterior of the wetted tube bank ($N_{\text{rows}}$ rows, transverse pitch $S_T$, longitudinal pitch $S_L$). Sensible heat conducted from the primary air into the thin falling water film evaporates water into the secondary stream.

2 2D Finite-Difference Cross-Flow Governing Equations

The tube bundle is discretized into a 2D mesh of $N_x \times N_y$ control volumes, where $i \in [1, N_x]$ marches along tube length $\Delta x = L / N_x$, and $j \in [1, N_y]$ marches across tube rows ($N_y = N_{\text{rows}}$).

Primary Air Energy Balance (Sensible)
$$T_{p}(i+1,j) = T_{p}(i,j) - \varepsilon_p \left[ T_{p}(i,j) - T_w(i,j) \right]$$ $$\varepsilon_p = 1 - \exp\left(-\text{NTU}_p\right), \quad \text{NTU}_p = \frac{UA_p}{\dot{m}_{p,\text{cell}} c_{p,a}}$$
Secondary Air Enthalpy & Mass Balance
$$h_s(i,j+1) = h_s(i,j) + \varepsilon_m \left[ h_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w) - h_s(i,j) \right]$$ $$\omega_s(i,j+1) = \omega_s(i,j) + \varepsilon_m \left[ \omega_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w) - \omega_s(i,j) \right]$$ $$\varepsilon_m = 1 - \exp\left(-\text{NTU}_m\right), \quad \text{NTU}_m = \frac{h_m A_o}{\dot{m}_{s,\text{cell}}}$$
Water Film Temperature Treatment (Three Modes)

Option A — Adiabatic Recirculated Energy Balance: Spray water has negligible net enthalpy change across the bundle; $T_w(i,j)$ is solved iteratively at every cell so primary sensible cooling equals secondary enthalpy gain:

$$\dot{m}_{p,\text{cell}} c_{p,a} \varepsilon_p \left[ T_{p}(i,j) - T_w(i,j) \right] = \dot{m}_{s,\text{cell}} \varepsilon_m \left[ h_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w(i,j)) - h_s(i,j) \right]$$

Option B — Supplied Water 3-Way Energy Balance ($T_{w,\text{in}}$ prescribed): Spray water enters at top row $j=1$ with user-specified $T_{w,\text{in}}$ and mass flow $\dot{m}_w$ ($\text{LPM}$), marching downward ($j = 1 \to N_{\text{rows}}$) against co-flow or counter-flow (bottom-to-top) secondary air:

$$\dot{m}_{w,\text{cell}} c_{p,w} \left[ T_w(i, j+1) - T_w(i, j) \right] = \dot{Q}_p(i,j) - \dot{Q}_s(i,j)$$

Option C — Constant Water Temperature ($T_w = T_{w,\text{const}}$): Treats the water film as isothermal across the entire tube bank (limiting case of very high $\dot{V}_w$).

3 Convective Heat & Mass Transfer Correlations

Overall Sensible Conductance ($UA_p$) & Nusselt Falling Film
$$\frac{1}{UA_p} = \frac{1}{h_p A_i} + \frac{\ln(D_o / D_i)}{2\pi k_w \Delta x N_{\text{col}}} + \frac{1}{h_{\text{film}} A_o}$$

From the spray water flow rate $\dot{V}_w$ ($\text{LPM}$), the wetting mass flow rate per unit length on both sides of the top tube row ($\Gamma = \frac{\dot{m}_w}{2 L N_{\text{cols}}}$) yields the Nusselt falling-film thickness $\delta_w$ and convective coefficient $h_{\text{film}}$:

$$\delta_w = \left( \frac{3 \mu_w \Gamma}{\rho_w^2 g} \right)^{1/3}, \quad Re_{\text{film}} = \frac{4\Gamma}{\mu_w}, \quad h_{\text{film}} = \max\!\left(\frac{k_w}{\delta_w},\; 0.822 \, k_w \left(\frac{\rho_w^2 g}{\mu_w^2}\right)^{1/3} Re_{\text{film}}^{-0.22}\right)$$
Internal Tube Flow (Gnielinski / Laminar)
$$Nu_p = \begin{cases} 3.66 & Re_p < 2300 \\ \dfrac{(f/8)(Re_p - 1000)Pr}{1 + 12.7\sqrt{f/8}(Pr^{2/3} - 1)} & Re_p \ge 2300 \end{cases}$$ $$f = (0.79 \ln Re_p - 1.64)^{-2}, \quad h_p = \frac{Nu_p k_{\text{air}}}{D_i}$$
Cross-Flow Tube Bank (Zukauskas + Lewis)
$$Nu_s = C \cdot Re_{\max}^m \cdot Pr^{0.36}, \quad h_s = \frac{Nu_s k_{\text{air}}}{D_o}$$ $$h_m = \frac{h_s}{c_{p,m} \, Le^{2/3}} \quad (Le \approx 0.865)$$

4 IEC Performance & Effectiveness Metrics

Sensible Cooling Capacity
$$\dot{Q}_{\text{sen}} = \dot{m}_p c_{p,a} (T_{p,\text{in}} - T_{p,\text{out}})$$
Wet-Bulb Effectiveness
$$\varepsilon_{wb} = \frac{T_{p,\text{in}} - T_{p,\text{out}}}{T_{p,\text{in}} - T_{wb,s,\text{in}}}$$
Dew-Point Effectiveness
$$\varepsilon_{dp} = \frac{T_{p,\text{in}} - T_{p,\text{out}}}{T_{p,\text{in}} - T_{dp,s,\text{in}}}$$

5 Lumped Whole-Bundle LMTD / LMED Method (Cross-Flow Correction $F$)

Instead of discretizing into $N_x \times N_y$ control volumes, the lumped method treats the entire tube bank ($A_{o,\text{tot}} = N_{\text{tot}}\pi D_o L$) as a single global exchanger using a representative water film temperature $T_w$ and the cross-flow correction factor $F$:

Global LMTD & LMED Driving Potentials
$$\dot{Q}_{\text{sen}} = F \cdot UA_{\text{tot}} \cdot \Delta T_{\text{lm}}, \quad \Delta T_{\text{lm}} = \frac{(T_{p,\text{in}} - T_w) - (T_{p,\text{out}} - T_w)}{\ln\left(\dfrac{T_{p,\text{in}} - T_w}{T_{p,\text{out}} - T_w}\right)}$$ $$\dot{Q}_{\text{sec}} = F \cdot h_m A_{o,\text{tot}} \cdot \Delta h_{\text{lm}}, \quad \Delta h_{\text{lm}} = \frac{(h_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w) - h_{s,\text{in}}) - (h_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w) - h_{s,\text{out}})}{\ln\left(\dfrac{h_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w) - h_{s,\text{in}}}{h_{s,\text{sat}}(T_w) - h_{s,\text{out}}}\right)}$$
Cross-Flow Correction Factor ($F$)

When $T_w$ is uniform across the bundle, $F = 1.0$. When $T_w$ varies spatially due to coupled primary-secondary energy balance, $F < 1$ accounts for cross-flow thermal non-uniformity via the unmixed-unmixed cross-flow effectiveness ratio ($P, R$):

$$F = \frac{\text{NTU}_{\text{counter-flow}}(P, R)}{\text{NTU}_{\text{cross-flow}}(P, R)} \le 1.0$$
